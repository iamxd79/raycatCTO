# Brand orientation

The project-local `public/images/brand/raycat-source.svg` is the canonical RayCat source. Its original full-image horizontal mirror transform has been removed; the embedded illustration is unchanged. `public/images/brand/raydium-original.png` is the supplied symbol reference. Never mirror branded illustrations to change their pose.

All active canonical image consumers use `canonical-corrected.webp`, including the homepage and Museum/Three.js textures. Browser and Apple icons were regenerated from the corrected source. The old mirrored WebP is retained only under `review/orientation`, outside public assets.

All 100 existing artworks were visually reviewed on five numbered contact sheets. No additional clearly reversed logos were identified; the original artworks and their Museum derivatives were preserved. Tiny or stylized marks in generated scenes cannot be certified for exact logo geometry from thumbnail inspection.

Future derivatives must use the project-local corrected source, not the mirrored Downloads SVG or historical generated scenes as logo references.
